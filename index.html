Muttch Lounge is an elegant private event venue inside Chatti, offering a refined setting for celebrations, corporate dinners, networking events and milestone occasions.

From private dining in Midtown Manhattan for a small table to a full restaurant buyout, our team shapes the room, the menu and the service around your guests.

Our craft

Cooked the long way, for your table

Every event menu at Muttch Lounge comes from the same kitchen and the same research as Chatti.

Recipes gathered at the source

Chef Regi spent more than three years travelling across Kerala, learning from home cooks, toddy shop chefs and culinary artisans.

Named for the clay pot

A chatti is the traditional clay pot at the heart of Kerala’s toddy shops and homes. It gives the restaurant its name and sets the tone for every menu we serve.

Made to be shared

Toddy shop dining is communal by nature: bold plates, passed around the table. Your event menu is built the same way, so every guest leaves having tasted a little of everything.
